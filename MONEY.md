# Temida Money

How Temida makes money. The company plan is `REVENUE.md` in the Code root; the ledger is `GTM.md`.

## Price and rail

Free on iPhone and Mac (ASC 6787857503) and free on the web at temida.jaybulb.com. Nothing is charged.

## What is actually sold

Nothing. Temida is a litigation organiser for people who go to court themselves. Case content lives only in each owner's account, never in this repo. The real harm is a leak. Case data must never be sold, shown or published.

## Who pays and why

Nobody. The people who use it are in court and need their own file in order. Charging them is the wrong trade.

## Cost to serve

No paid API found in the app. Accounts and sync run on jaybulb-api, on Cloudflare Workers and D1. Hosting is Cloudflare, with no paid service behind the app.

## Where we are

- 2026-10-19: renamed to Temida, because Themis is taken on the store. The store listing changed with 1.1.0.
- 2026-10-19: the repo went public from one clean commit. The old history lives in a private repo.
- 2026-08-27: version 1.0.3 was pulled from sale after screenshots showed case data. It was restored once 1.0.3 cleared review.
- Revenue: no verified number (the App Store Connect key cannot read sales yet).

## Role in the company plan

Free on purpose, a public good. GTM.md lists it that way. It serves no revenue stage. It is the trust case for the fleet: a legal tool that charges no one.

## Next

None for money. Keep it free, and keep case data out of the repo.

## Million, billion, trillion

**A million.** Temida is the free organiser for people who go to court alone, and it still charges nobody.

**A billion.** Temida would be the free court file tool in every province, with case data kept in each person's own account.

**A trillion.** Temida would be a public service the platform runs for free, with the money coming from somewhere else.
