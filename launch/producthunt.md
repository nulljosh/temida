## First comment

Hi, I'm Josh, I built Temida.

I ended up representing myself in a court case and found out fast that the hard part isn't the law, it's the paper. Grounds, witness names, damages math, deadlines, call scripts, all of it scattered across notes apps, email drafts, and a manila folder that never made it to the right meeting. Temida is the tool I wished existed: one place for a case, split into Case (facts, witnesses, grounds, journal), Money (damages scenarios, settlement comparables), and Actions (lawyer contacts, timeline, checklist, call scripts).

Everything syncs across web, iPhone, and Mac through your own account, and Face ID locks the iPhone app. Each account only ever sees its own case data, nothing is shared or pooled. It's for self-represented litigants and anyone managing a small claims, family, or municipal matter without hiring counsel for the paperwork side.

It's a narrow tool built from one real case, not a legal-advice product and not trying to be a law firm in an app. If you're already deep in your own case, or helping someone who is, I'd like to hear what's missing.

Free. Happy to answer anything.
